Marketing Support Options

All of our authors follow the same underlying strategy. The main difference is how much you want to do yourself.

Option 1

Marketing Strategy & Consultation

For authors who want clarity and a plan, but don’t need execution support.

This is for you if you’re capable, motivated, and just want to know what to focus on.

Includes:

  • A Book Positioning + Message Spine
  • Clear guidance on who the book is for and what it’s really about
  • Platform recommendation. LinkedIn or Instagram
  • A 90-day marketing roadmap
  • Pre-launch, launch, and post-launch sequencing
  • Three 30-minute strategy calls

Option 2

Done-With-You Marketing

For authors who want support, structure, and accountability, but still want to be the face of their work.


This is the most common option.

Includes:

  • Everything in Strategy & Consultation
  • A 90-day content plan tied directly to your book
  • Support turning the book into a public thinking project
  • Weekly or bi-weekly feedback on posts and newsletters
  • Launch planning and messaging
  • Outreach guidance for podcasts and communities
  • Collaborative setup of core marketing assets

Option 3

Done-For-You Marketing

Best for authors who want the system running without managing every moving part.

This is a high-touch option for busy authors.

Includes:

  • Content strategy and execution on your behalf
  • Newsletter writing and scheduling
  • Launch coordination and messaging
  • Outreach support and follow-ups
  • Light paid ads during launch and post-launch
  • Full setup of core marketing assets
