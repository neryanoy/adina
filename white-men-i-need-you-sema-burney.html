"White Men, I Need You"
by Sema Burney

Most DEI books are written for the people already in the room. This one is for the people who run it.

Sema Burney has spent over two decades in the trenches of inclusion work. And she's learned one uncomfortable truth:

the fastest path to equity runs straight through white men.
White Men, I Need You! is the guide for you if you want to stop nodding along and start making things different.

About the Author

Sema Burney is a DEI consultant, facilitator, and speaker with 20+ years of experience helping leaders and organizations build more inclusive workplaces. With a background in accounting and an MBA, she brings a practical, results-driven approach to navigating complex conversations around power, privilege, and belonging.
