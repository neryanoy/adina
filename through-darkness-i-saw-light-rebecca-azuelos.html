Through Darkness I Saw Light
by Rebecca Azuelos

On an ordinary September morning, Rebecca Azuelos received the call every parent fears. Her son, Shalom Nethanel Ouanounou, had suffered a catastrophic asthma attack.

What followed was a six-month journey through faith, grief, hope, miracles, and unconditional love. Refusing to leave her son’s side, Rebecca discovered that even in the darkest moments, light can still be found.

Be the first to know when the book drops.

About the Author

Rebecca Azuelos is a devoted mother, community volunteer, and advocate for kindness. After losing her son, Shalom Nethanel Ouanounou, she dedicated herself to preserving his legacy through service and charity.
